TCHEAD: Text-To-Animatable Gaussian Head via Texture-Consistent Diffusion Guidance

Paper Code coming soon! arXiv

Abstract

Text-based generation of animatable 3D head avatars has emerged as a crucial frontier for immersive applications in gaming, film, and social media. Existing methods typically combine 3D Morphable Models (3DMMs) with 2D diffusion priors to generate animatable head avatars. However, they struggle to synthesize fine-grained appearance details and achieve accurate driving due to ambiguities in 2D diffusion predictions. To improve appearance detail and animation accuracy, we present TCHead, a novel text-based animatable Gaussian head generation framework based on Noise-Consistent Score Distillation (NCSD) and UV-guided Cross-Pose Refinement (UV-CPR). Specifically, NCSD samples shared noise on the canonical FLAME surface to generate consistent noise for different views and expressions to mitigate ambiguity in 2D diffusion guidance. UV-CPR uses UV-aligned appearance differences to guide the final diffusion denoising step and refine the avatar, reducing cross-pose appearance inconsistencies and enhancing texture details. Experiments show that TCHead outperforms existing methods in generating animatable 3D head avatars from text.

Method

Overview of TCHead, a novel framework for generating animatable Gaussian heads from text. (a) FLAME-UVD Initialization constructs a FLAME-bound Gaussian avatar from a text-generated portrait. Gaussians are parameterized by UV coordinates and normal displacements on the FLAME surface, establishing canonical surface correspondences while inheriting FLAME's facial animation capability. (b) Noise-Consistent Score Distillation (NCSD) reduces inconsistent appearance across views, poses, and expressions while recovering missing details by constructing a shared noise map. (c) UV-guided Cross-Pose Refinement (UV-CPR) aligns reference images with the target view using canonical UV correspondences. It measures their appearance difference and uses the resulting gradient to update the latent before the final denoising step. The diffusion model remains frozen, and the refined images are then used to optimize the 3DGS.
